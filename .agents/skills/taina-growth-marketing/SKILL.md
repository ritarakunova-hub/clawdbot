# TAINA Growth Marketing

Build a focused, measurable go-to-market system for a small founder-led B2B product. Optimize for qualified conversations and product activation, not vanity reach.

## Source of truth
Read the current landing page, launch plan, product limitations, brand assets, and actual channel history. Preserve TAINA's dark plum, warm gold, wine, and dusty rose visual language unless the user explicitly changes it.

## Workflow
1. Select one ICP and one use case per campaign.
2. State the problem in the buyer's language; lead with costly workflow friction, not AI buzzwords.
3. Make every claim traceable to a product capability, customer evidence, or cited research.
4. Create one primary asset and repurpose it into Telegram, VK, YouTube Shorts, and email. Do not create separate content machinery for every channel.
5. Use founder-led demos, problem teardown, before/after process maps, and real pilot learning as proof. Do not fabricate case studies.
6. Give each asset one CTA and tag links with UTMs where possible.
7. Track qualified leads, applications, show-up rate, pilot starts, activation, time-to-first-value, paid conversion, and acquisition source.
8. Run one variable-changing experiment at a time; document hypothesis, audience, date, result, and decision.

## Content pillars
- Knowledge trapped in employees' heads
- Process ownership and undocumented exceptions
- Fragmented sources and version confusion
- One-process diagnostic walkthroughs
- Honest V1 boundaries and safe use
- Findings from real pilot interviews

## Rules
- Do not recommend paid ads before message and activation are validated.
- Do not optimize only for views, likes, or subscribers.
- Do not promise savings or ROI without measurement.
- Never imply Knowledge Map includes RAG, AI parsing, team sync, or cloud storage.
- Keep the founder's workload low: one weekly core idea, then reuse.
